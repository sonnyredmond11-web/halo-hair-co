Shipping & Returns

Free UK Delivery

Delivery Information

  • Free next-day tracked delivery on all UK orders over £80
  • Standard UK delivery (2–3 days) — £4.95 on orders under £80
  • Next-day delivery guaranteed when ordered before 2pm Monday–Friday
  • Orders placed on weekends are dispatched the following Monday
  • International delivery available — rates calculated at checkout
  • EU delivery: 3–5 working days
  • Rest of World: 5–10 working days

All orders are dispatched from our London warehouse and sent via Royal Mail Tracked or DPD. You will receive a tracking number by email once your order has shipped.

30-Day Returns

Returns Policy

We want you to love your Halo Hair Co extensions. If for any reason you are not completely satisfied, we offer a 30-day returns policy on all unworn, unopened items.

  • Items must be returned within 30 days of delivery
  • Extensions must be unworn, in their original packaging with tags attached
  • For hygiene reasons, we cannot accept returns on worn or used hair extensions
  • Sale items are non-refundable but can be exchanged
  • Return postage is the customer's responsibility unless the item is faulty

To start a return, please contact us with your order number and reason for return. We will respond within 24 hours with return instructions.

Exchanges

Want a different shade or length? We're happy to exchange unworn extensions within 30 days. Contact our team with your order number and we'll arrange a free exchange on your first order.

Not sure which shade is right for you? Use our free Shade Matching service before you buy.

Faulty Items

In the unlikely event that your extensions arrive damaged or faulty, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the issue. We will arrange a free replacement or full refund at no cost to you.
