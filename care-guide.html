Care Guide

Your Halo Hair Co extensions are made from 100% Remy human hair and can be styled, washed and cared for just like your natural hair. With the right care, your extensions will look beautiful for 6–12 months or longer.

01

Wash Gently

Wash 1–2 times per week with sulphate-free shampoo. Never rub — always smooth downward.

02

Condition Always

Apply conditioner from mid-length to ends. Leave for 3–5 minutes before rinsing.

03

Dry Carefully

Pat dry with a soft towel. Never wring or twist. Air dry when possible.

04

Brush Daily

Use a soft bristle brush, starting from the ends and working upward to avoid tangles.

Washing

  • Wash extensions 1–2 times per week — overwashing strips natural oils
  • Always use sulphate-free, alcohol-free shampoo and conditioner
  • Wash in lukewarm water — never hot
  • Apply shampoo in a downward motion, never circular
  • Rinse thoroughly to remove all product residue
  • Apply a deep conditioning mask once a week for extra softness

Heat Styling

  • Extensions can be blow-dried, curled and straightened up to 230°C
  • Always apply a heat protectant spray before using hot tools
  • Avoid applying heat directly to clips, bonds or weft seams
  • Use a lower heat setting where possible to extend the life of your extensions
  • Allow extensions to cool before brushing after heat styling

Sleeping & Storage

  • Remove clip-in extensions before sleeping
  • Braid hair loosely before bed if wearing semi-permanent extensions
  • Sleep on a silk or satin pillowcase to reduce friction and tangling
  • Store clip-ins flat in their original box or a soft storage bag
  • Keep extensions away from direct sunlight and humidity when not in use

Colouring

Our extensions can be toned and coloured darker, but we strongly recommend having this done by a professional stylist experienced in working with extensions. We cannot be held responsible for damage caused by colouring at home.
