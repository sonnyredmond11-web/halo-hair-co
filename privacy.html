Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2024

Who We Are

Halo Hair Co Ltd operates halohairco.co.uk. We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and store your information when you visit our website or make a purchase.

What Data We Collect

  • Name and email address when you place an order or contact us
  • Delivery address and phone number for order fulfilment
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe, never stored by us
  • Browsing data and cookies to improve your experience on our site
  • Communications you send us via our contact form

How We Use Your Data

  • To process and fulfil your orders
  • To send order confirmations and shipping updates
  • To respond to your enquiries
  • To send marketing emails — only with your consent, and you can unsubscribe at any time
  • To improve our website and services

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time. To make a request, contact us at our contact page. We will respond within 30 days.

  • Right to access the data we hold about you
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to request deletion of your data
  • Right to withdraw marketing consent at any time

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep our site functioning. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us. Halo Hair Co Ltd, London, United Kingdom.
