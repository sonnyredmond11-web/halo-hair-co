Our Story

Made with intention.
Worn with confidence.

We started Halo Hair Co because we believed the extensions market was broken — cheap quality passed off as luxury, with no transparency about sourcing. We decided to do it differently.

The Beginning

A salon owner's obsession became a brand.

Our founder spent over a decade working in London's most prestigious salons. She saw, again and again, clients spending hundreds on extensions that tangled, faded, and fell apart within weeks.

In 2024, she set out to source the finest Remy human hair — travelling to find ethical suppliers who shared her commitment to quality and transparency. Halo Hair Co was born from that obsession.

Every set of extensions we sell has been selected, tested and approved by our in-house stylist team before it ever reaches your door.

What We Stand For

Our Values

01

Ethical Sourcing

We work only with suppliers who share our standards. Every strand of hair is sourced from consenting donors, fairly compensated, through fully audited supply chains. No shortcuts, ever.

02

Uncompromising Quality

We use only 100% Remy human hair — cuticles intact, aligned in the same direction, to prevent tangling and ensure the most natural movement. Heat-safe up to 230°C.

03

Radical Transparency

We tell you exactly what you're buying. Origin, weight, length, method. No vague "premium quality" claims — just honest product information so you can choose with confidence.
