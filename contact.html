Common Questions

Quick Answers

How does free shade matching work?

Simply send us a photo of your natural hair in natural daylight — no flash, no filters. Our stylists will recommend up to three shades within 4 hours, Monday to Friday.

How long does delivery take?

All UK orders are shipped next-day tracked via Royal Mail Special Delivery, dispatched same day on orders placed before 1pm. International orders take 3–5 working days.

What is your returns policy?

We accept returns of unworn, unsealed extensions within 30 days of purchase. Contact us and we'll arrange a prepaid return label — no questions asked.

Are the extensions heat-friendly?

Yes. All Halo Hair Co extensions are 100% Remy human hair and can be styled with heat tools up to 230°C, just like your natural hair. We recommend a heat protector spray.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

We have an established trade programme for salons and stylists. Contact us via the form above with your salon details and we'll be in touch with our trade pricing guide.

How do I know my extensions are authentic?

Every set ships with a unique authentication code. Visit our Verify page to confirm your extensions are genuine Halo Hair Co products.
